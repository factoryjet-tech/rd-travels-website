Cars, Tempo Travellers & Buses, with driver.

This page is the canonical visual reference for the RD Travels design system. White-dominant (70%) with saffron accents (30%), single-family typography (Plus Jakarta Sans), and explicit vehicle-with-driver messaging. Upload this HTML, the linked CSS, and screenshots of this page to Claude Design.

01 · Color

White (70%) & saffron (30%).

Backgrounds dominate, saffron punctuates. The ratio is non-negotiable — inverting it reads as advertising or "sale," not "operator."

02 · Typography

One family, eight weights.

Plus Jakarta Sans handles everything from 80px display to 12px form labels. Hierarchy comes from weight, size, and color — not a second typeface. Designed by Tokotype (Indonesia, 2020) — South Asian regional context built in, excellent tabular numerals for pricing tables.

03 · Buttons & chips

Verb + object. Always.

Buttons describe what happens, not what to click. "Submit," "Click here," and "Learn more" are forbidden. Saffron primary, WhatsApp green for chat, outline for secondary, ghost-text-with-arrow for tertiary.

09 · Do & don't

The drift the model always tries to make.

Default outputs from large language models slide toward "AI startup template" and "luxury hospitality." This brand is neither. When the design drifts, return here.

Do

  • White surfaces dominate (70%); saffron punctuates (30%)
  • Plus Jakarta Sans for everything; weight contrast for hierarchy
  • Saffron color on accent phrase — not italic, not serif
  • Every vehicle mentions "with driver"
  • Tabular numerals on all prices (₹11/km, ₹2,400, ₹6,800)
  • Verb-object button labels ("Get instant quote")
  • Hairline dividers and warm-tinted shadows
  • Mobile-first; sticky WhatsApp + Call CTA at the bottom

Don't

  • Editorial luxury (Mr & Mrs Smith / Fraunces serif / italic flourishes)
  • Aggregator UI (yellow CTAs, dense filter rails, "999 happy customers")
  • AI startup defaults (Inter, purple gradients, glassmorphism)
  • Stock travel photography (sunsets, drone shots, generic couples)
  • Pure black text — always use --color-ink
  • "Submit" / "Click here" / "Learn more" buttons
  • Saffron as a large background block (it's spice, not the meal)
  • Parallax, marquees, or scroll-jacking
