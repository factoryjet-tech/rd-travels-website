### Section 2 — Founder story (long-form prose)

**Eyebrow:** THE WHY

**H2:** Why Ronak started [accent]RD Travels.[/accent]

**Body (6 paragraphs):**

In 2015, Ronak Dineshbhai Barot was running a small travels desk near Ahmedabad's CG Road, mostly handling airport drops and weekend Saurashtra trips. He noticed a pattern that bothered him — the customers he spoke to weren't talking to operators. They were talking to layers of brokers. The price they paid was rarely the price the actual operator received. The driver who showed up was rarely the one who'd been promised. The vehicle that arrived was rarely the one in the photo.

The Indian travels industry had become an aggregator stack — a customer at the top, three intermediaries in the middle, and an operator at the bottom doing the actual work for the smallest cut. Information lost at every layer. Markup added at every layer. Accountability evaporating at every layer.

Ronak saw an opening that wasn't really an opening — it was just a return to how travels businesses had always worked before the broker layer. A direct operator. One yard, one phone number, one person who picked up when something went wrong on the highway at 2am. The customer talks to the people who own the vehicles and employ the drivers. The price they're quoted is the price the operator is paid. No hidden cuts, no telephone-game requirements.

He started with one Maruti Ertiga, a Tourist Permit, and a notebook. The first month he ran fourteen trips. The second month, thirty. By the end of year one, he'd added a 12-seater Tempo Traveller for a friend's family pilgrimage to Somnath. By year three, he'd added a second Tempo and an Innova. By year five — 2020, the year nobody wants to remember — he had a small Volvo coach sitting idle for nine months and made a decision to keep paying his drivers regardless.

Today, RD Travels runs a fleet that ranges from sedans to 56-seater Volvos, employs drivers (not contractors), and books most of its work through WhatsApp because that's how Ahmedabad actually operates. The customer base is roughly half repeat — wedding families who used us for a daughter's marriage and now use us for a son's, corporate procurement teams who put us on their preferred-vendor list after the first GST-clear invoice, NRI families whose parents only trust one number for transport during their India visits.

The promise hasn't changed since 2015. Direct from the operator. Owned fleet, employed drivers. GST-clear pricing. No broker layer. If something goes wrong on a trip, Ronak's phone still rings — most months, it doesn't, but the fact that it can is the difference. That's RD Travels.

**Items (7, accordion):**

#### Q: What's the fastest channel to reach you?
A: WhatsApp during business hours (9 AM – 8 PM Mon-Sat) — typical reply in 5–15 minutes. After hours, WhatsApp messages are answered by 9 AM the next day. For active-trip emergencies, the phone line is 24×7.

#### Q: Are you open on Sundays?
A: Yes, 10 AM – 6 PM. Booking team is smaller on Sundays so reply times stretch slightly. Active-trip support is 24×7 every day of the year.

#### Q: Can I book same-day?
A: Often, yes — depends on vehicle availability. For airport drops with 4+ hours notice, almost always yes. For multi-day tours or 56-seater Volvos, book 48–72 hours ahead. WhatsApp us with the request and we'll confirm availability immediately.

#### Q: How far in advance should I book for weddings?
A: For wedding transport (multiple vehicles, specific timings, decorated bridal car), book 4–8 weeks ahead. For peak wedding season (Nov–Feb), 8–12 weeks is safer. Last-minute is possible but vehicle choice narrows.

#### Q: Do you provide an airport placard with my name?
A: Yes — for arrivals at Mumbai BOM, Ahmedabad AMD, or any other airport, we send the driver with a printed name placard. Confirm the arrival flight number when booking and we'll have the driver waiting at the appropriate gate.

#### Q: Do you have a separate corporate booking desk?
A: Yes — for corporate retainers, multi-vehicle bookings, and recurring transport contracts, email hello@rdtravels.in and we'll route to the corporate desk. GST-itemised invoicing, monthly billing, and dedicated account manager available for accounts above a minimum monthly volume.

#### Q: What if there's a problem during the trip?
A: Phone line is 24×7. Driver has direct access to dispatch. For major issues — vehicle breakdown, driver concern, route problem — escalation goes to the founder's number. In ten years, escalations have been rare; the fact that the path exists is what matters.
