Do & don't — the drift the model always tries to make

Do

  • White surfaces dominate (70%); saffron punctuates (30%)
  • One family — Switzer, weight contrast for hierarchy
  • Saffron color on accent phrase — not italic, not serif
  • Every vehicle says "with driver"
  • Tabular numerals on prices (₹11/km, ₹2,400)
  • Verb-object button labels ("Get instant quote")
  • Hairline dividers, warm-tinted shadows

Don't

  • Editorial luxury (Mr & Mrs Smith, Fraunces, italic flourishes)
  • Aggregator UI (yellow CTAs, "999 happy customers")
  • AI-startup defaults (Inter, purple gradients, glassmorphism)
  • Stock travel photography (sunsets, drone shots)
  • Pure black text — always use --color-ink
  • "Submit" / "Click here" / "Learn more" buttons
  • Saffron as a large background block (it's spice, not the meal)
