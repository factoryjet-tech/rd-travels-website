One-Way Outstation Cab

Pay only for the distance from Ahmedabad to your destination. No return distance charged. Formula: per-km rate × one-way distance + tolls + driver bata (if overnight). Ideal when you're flying back, taking a train, or have an open return date.

Round Trip Outstation Cab

Pay for both legs of the journey upfront. Formula: per-km rate × (distance × 2) + tolls + driver bata. The advantage: only one set of driver bata applies if the return is within the same overnight halt. Multi-day round trips qualify for special package rates — ask on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Outstation Cab from Ahmedabad — Common Questions

Answers to the questions most people ask before booking an outstation taxi from Ahmedabad.

What is the outstation cab rate from Ahmedabad? +

RDB Travels charges ₹11/km for a sedan (Maruti Dzire / Toyota Etios), ₹13/km for an MPV (Maruti Ertiga), and ₹16/km for an SUV (Toyota Innova / Innova Crysta). These are the final per-km rates — no platform fees or surge pricing added on top. Tolls are charged at actuals and driver bata is ₹400/night if an overnight halt is required.

How much does an Ahmedabad to Mumbai cab cost? +

An Ahmedabad to Mumbai one-way cab in a Maruti Dzire costs approximately ~₹5,800 (525 km × ₹11/km + driver bata ₹400 + tolls at actuals). For an Innova Crysta with 7 passengers, the one-way fare is approximately ~₹8,400 + tolls. Round-trip quotes for Mumbai are available at a combined rate that saves on driver bata if returning within a day.

Is one-way cab available from Ahmedabad? +

Yes — RDB Travels offers true one-way outstation cabs from Ahmedabad. You pay only for the one-way distance. No return-leg charge is levied. Formula: per-km rate × one-way km + tolls at actuals + driver bata (₹400/night if overnight). This is ideal when you're returning by flight, train, or on a different date.

Are tolls included in the per-km rate? +

No — tolls are charged separately at actuals, which is the transparent approach. You see exactly what toll plazas were paid and at what amount. Toll receipts are provided on request. Bundling tolls into the per-km rate is common among aggregators who then quote a higher base rate — our approach keeps the base rate clean and honest.

What is driver bata and when does it apply? +

Driver bata is a night halt allowance of ₹400 per night, paid to the driver when he stays at the outstation destination overnight. It does not apply on same-day return trips or day trips where the driver returns to Ahmedabad the same evening. On multi-day trips (e.g., a 3-day Saurashtra pilgrimage), bata applies for each overnight halt — this will be itemised in your quote.

Is RDB Travels better than Savaari or Ola Outstation? +

RDB Travels is a direct operator — not a platform. Savaari and Ola Outstation charge a 15–20% platform fee on top of the rate you see, and assign drivers from a pool where quality can vary trip to trip. With RDB Travels, the rate quoted on WhatsApp is the final amount, the driver is known in advance, and Ronak Barot (the owner) is the contact for the entire journey — no call center intermediary.

Do all vehicles have All India Tourist Permit (AITP)? +

Yes. All outstation vehicles operated by RDB Travels carry AITP — the All India Tourist Permit required for interstate cab operations. This permit is checked at state border checkpoints between Gujarat, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Madhya Pradesh. Without AITP, a vehicle can be detained at the border — this is a risk with locally-permitted cars hired through informal channels.

How is round trip outstation pricing calculated? +

Round trip fare = per-km rate × (one-way distance × 2) + tolls (both ways) + driver bata (one night if the return is next day). For multi-day round trips with multiple stops, a package rate is usually more economical — send your itinerary on WhatsApp and we'll calculate it precisely. Two separate one-way bookings are generally more expensive than a single round trip booking.

Can I book an outstation cab from Ahmedabad airport directly? +

Yes — RDB Travels can pick you up from Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD) and drive directly to any outstation city — Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Udaipur, or Mumbai. Pre-book with your flight number and estimated arrival time. The outstation per-km rate applies from the airport. This avoids the cost and time of an in-city taxi plus a separate outstation booking.

What outstation pilgrimage routes do you cover from Ahmedabad? +

RDB Travels covers all major Gujarat and Rajasthan pilgrimage destinations: Dwarka (455 km, ~₹5,000 Dzire), Somnath (~420 km, ~₹5,000 Dzire), Ambaji (~180 km, ~₹5,000 Dzire), Palitana (~215 km, ~₹5,000 Dzire), and Mount Abu (220 km, ~₹5,000 Dzire). Multi-day pilgrimage packages for Char Dham Gujarat circuits are also available. WhatsApp for a package quote.
