Frequently Asked Questions

Ahmedabad Airport Taxi — Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about booking a fixed-rate airport cab at SVPI Airport (AMD), Ahmedabad.

What is the fixed rate for an airport taxi in Ahmedabad? +

RDB Travels charges fixed airport transfer rates regardless of traffic or time of day. Sedan (Dzire/Etios): from ₹650. MPV (Ertiga): from ₹850. SUV (Innova/Innova Crysta): from ₹1,100. These cover transfers within Ahmedabad city — no surge, no hidden extras except toll at actuals. Outstation transfers are billed at a per-km rate: ₹12/km (Sedan), ₹16/km (MPV), ₹18/km (SUV).

Does RDB Travels charge extra if my flight is delayed? +

No. Your driver tracks your flight in real time using your flight number. If your IndiGo, Air India, Vistara, or international flight is delayed, the driver adjusts the pickup schedule accordingly — no waiting charge, no cancellation fee. You only pay the agreed fixed fare quoted at the time of booking.

Is there a meet and greet at Ahmedabad airport? +

Yes — for every airport pickup, your driver waits at the arrivals exit with a name board displaying your name. For Terminal 2 international arrivals, the driver waits at the international arrivals gate. Meet and greet is standard and there is no extra charge for this service.

Which terminal does Ahmedabad airport use? +

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (IATA: AMD) in Hansol has two terminals. Terminal 1 handles select domestic routes. Terminal 2 handles all international arrivals and departures, plus most domestic IndiGo, Air India, and other airline flights. When you share your flight number with RDB Travels, your driver will identify the correct terminal automatically.

Can I book a cab from AMD airport directly to Surat or Vadodara? +

Yes. RDB Travels offers outstation transfers straight from Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport — you do not need to go into the city first. AMD Airport to Surat (~260 km) from ₹3,800 by Sedan. AMD Airport to Vadodara (~110 km) from ₹1,800. AMD Airport to Rajkot (~215 km) from ₹3,200. All at fixed per-km rates with no surge.

Does RDB Travels cover early morning and late night airport transfers? +

Yes. RDB Travels operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Early morning departures at 3–4 AM and late-night international arrivals after midnight are handled routinely. Unlike Uber or Ola which typically surge 2–3× at peak airport hours and early morning slots, RDB Travels charges the same fixed fare regardless of the time of day or night.

Why is RDB Travels better than Uber or Ola for airport transfers? +

Three key differences: (1) Fixed fare — Uber and Ola surge 2–3× at peak airport hours. RDB Travels quotes one fixed price before you book. (2) Reliability — app-based drivers cancel last minute, especially at odd hours; your RDB Travels driver is confirmed the night before. (3) GST invoice — RDB Travels provides a full GST-registered tax invoice on every booking, which individual Uber/Ola drivers often cannot.

How do I book an airport cab with RDB Travels? +

WhatsApp +91 70210 55109 with: your flight number and arrival/departure time, pickup or drop address, number of passengers and bags, and vehicle preference. You'll receive a fixed fare quote within 15 minutes. Driver name and contact number are confirmed the evening before your trip. No app to download, no account needed.

Does RDB Travels provide a GST invoice for airport cabs? +

Yes. RDB Travels is a GST-registered transport operator based in Ahmedabad. Every airport cab booking — whether a city transfer or outstation trip from AMD — includes a proper GST invoice suitable for corporate expense reimbursement and business accounting. This is not available from most individual Uber, Ola, or Rapido drivers.

How far is Ahmedabad airport from the city and what does it cost? +

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD) is in Hansol, approximately 16 km north of Ahmedabad city centre (CG Road / Navrangpura). Travel time is 25–40 minutes depending on traffic. To SG Highway hotels the airport is about 18–22 km (35–50 minutes). To Gandhinagar it's around 25 km (30–40 minutes). RDB Travels covers all of these at a flat fixed rate from ₹650 for a Sedan — no meter, no surge.
