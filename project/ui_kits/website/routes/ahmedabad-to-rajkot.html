Rajkot — and what comes next

NH 27 from Ahmedabad runs west through Botad and into Rajkot — a straightforward 4-hour drive on a well-maintained national highway. Rajkot itself is Saurashtra's commercial and administrative centre, with a busy industrial area and the new Rajkot Smart City under development.

Most day-trip bookings are corporate — meetings at Rajkot industrial GIDC, trading visits, or government-related travel. The driver waits and returns the same day.

Extending to Saurashtra pilgrimage: Rajkot is the ideal staging point for a full Saurashtra circuit — Dwarka (240 km), Bet Dwarka, Somnath (200 km from Rajkot), and Palitana (220 km via Bhavnagar). Many families book a 4–5 day circuit out of Ahmedabad covering all major Saurashtra dhams with the same driver throughout.

Unlike platforms like Savaari, which reassign drivers per leg, RDB Travels assigns one driver for the full multi-day Saurashtra circuit. You build familiarity with the person taking you to these sacred sites — which matters for many families.

FAQ

Honest answers, upfront.

Everything you'd want to know before you book — answered directly.

How much does Ahmedabad to Rajkot cab cost?+

Sedan from ₹11/km — approximately ₹2,365 base fare, plus tolls, driver bata, and 5% GST. WhatsApp for exact itemised quote.

How long is Ahmedabad to Rajkot by cab?+

~4 hours on NH 27 under normal conditions. Road quality is good through Botad; Rajkot city entry can add 20–30 minutes during peak hours.

Can I extend from Rajkot to Dwarka?+

Yes — Dwarka is ~240 km from Rajkot. Many bookings continue Ahmedabad → Rajkot → Dwarka → Somnath → Ahmedabad as a 3–4 day pilgrimage circuit on the same vehicle.

Do you cover Rajkot Airport drops?+

Yes — Rajkot Airport (RAJ) drops from Ahmedabad. Driver plans departure backwards from boarding time.

What's a typical Saurashtra pilgrimage circuit cost?+

A 4-day circuit (Ahmedabad → Dwarka → Somnath → Palitana → Ahmedabad, ~1,200–1,400 km) in an Innova typically costs ₹19,000–₹24,000 including driver bata and GST, plus tolls at actuals. WhatsApp for a specific quote.

Can I book a Tempo Traveller for a group yatra?+

Yes — 12-seat and 17-seat Tempo Travellers are popular for family and society yatras to Saurashtra dhams. The TT fits 10–16 pilgrims plus luggage comfortably.
