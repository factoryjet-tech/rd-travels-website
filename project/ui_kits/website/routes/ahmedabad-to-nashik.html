Route: Ahmedabad → Surat → Vapi → Nashik

Ahmedabad→ Surat→ Vapi→ Dahanu→ Nashik

From Vapi, the route diverges from NH 48 toward Nashik via NH 848 and the Kasara Ghat road. The Ghat section — from Manor toward Igatpuri and down to Nashik — is scenic but requires careful driving: our drivers on this route are specifically those comfortable with Ghat sections.

From Nashik, popular extensions: Trimbakeshwar (30 km), Shirdi (90 km from Nashik), Shani Shingnapur (100 km), and Bhimashankar (150 km). For Shirdi visitors coming from Ahmedabad, Nashik can be combined into a 3–4 day pilgrimage circuit.

Nashik pilgrimage groups — what RDB does differently

Pilgrimage group travel requires more than just a big vehicle. It requires a driver who is patient, knows the temple parking areas, understands that darshan timing is non-negotiable, and won't rush the group. Our Tempo Traveller drivers on the Nashik–Shirdi pilgrimage route are specifically selected for this. It's a different skill set from airport drops.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Ahmedabad to Nashik cab cost?

~580 km on NH 48. Sedan from ₹11/km outstation. Ghat road sections don't add to the distance but require a driver experienced on those roads. WhatsApp for exact quote with vehicle options and your dates.

Can I combine Nashik and Shirdi from Ahmedabad?

Very commonly done. Ahmedabad → Nashik (overnight) → Trimbakeshwar darshan → Shirdi → return to Ahmedabad or extend. Total circuit: 1,100–1,200 km over 3–4 days. WhatsApp us your itinerary and we'll quote the full circuit.

Ahmedabad to Nashik by Tempo Traveller for pilgrimage group?

Yes — one of our most-booked formats. 12-seater and 17-seater Tempo Travellers for society and neighbourhood pilgrimage groups. Driver briefed on pilgrimage schedule, temple parking, and early-morning departure requirements.

Is the Kasara Ghat road safe for Tempo Travellers?

Yes, with an experienced driver. The Ghat section is a regular Nashik approach route. Our drivers who take Tempo Travellers on this route have done the Ghat multiple times. We do not send first-time Ghat drivers on group bookings.
