Frequently Asked Questions

What is the bus hire rate in Ahmedabad?

₹32–₹45/km for a 32–35 seater mini bus, ₹42–₹55/km for a 45-seater coach, and ₹55–₹70/km for a Volvo 56-seater — depending on spec and route. Local city day rates start from ₹9,000 for a 32-seater (100 km / 10 hr slab). All quotes include driver bata and GST.

How many passengers does a 56-seater Volvo hold?

Our Volvo B7R and B9R coaches seat 41 to 56 passengers with individual recliner seats, overhead luggage bins, and AC. For groups of 45–55, this is the most comfortable and cost-effective option per seat. A second-driver arrangement is available for overnight journeys over 600 km.

Can I hire a bus from Ahmedabad to Mumbai for a large group?

Yes — Ahmedabad to Mumbai is one of our most common group bookings. ~525 km on NH 48, roughly 9–10 hours. A 45-seater costs approximately ₹24,000–₹30,000 one-way depending on vehicle and timing. For groups of 30+, a single coach often works out cheaper per head than booking multiple cabs or Tempo Travellers.

Do you provide buses for school picnics and college trips?

Yes — school and college trips are one of our most regular booking types. We provide a GST invoice that institutions can submit for reimbursement. Our drivers are experienced with school-group bookings and follow appropriate safety protocols. Advance booking is important during school holiday seasons and weekends.

Is RDB Travels a direct operator or a booking platform?

Direct operator. RDB Travels owns its fleet and employs its drivers. Unlike Savaari, redBus charter, or other aggregators that connect you to third-party operators, booking with RDB means you deal with the owner directly. Same contact from quote to drop.

How far in advance should I book a bus?

Weekday corporate or school trips: 3–5 days notice is usually fine. Weddings, pilgrimages, and peak-season routes (Rann Utsav Oct–Feb, Navratri): book 2–4 weeks ahead. Large fleet bookings of 3 or more buses: 10–14 days minimum. WhatsApp to check availability for your date.

What's the difference between a bus and a Tempo Traveller — which should I book?

A Tempo Traveller (12–26 seats) is more manoeuvrable on narrow roads, ghats, and village access routes — good for pilgrimage circuits and hilly terrain. A bus (32–56 seats) is more economical per seat for larger groups on highway routes and offers more luggage space. If your group is 28–35, a 32–35 seater mini bus is often the right call. WhatsApp us your group size and route — we'll tell you exactly which vehicle makes sense.
