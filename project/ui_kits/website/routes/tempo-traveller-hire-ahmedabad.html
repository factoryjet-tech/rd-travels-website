Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Tempo Traveller hire rate in Ahmedabad?

RDB Travels charges ₹20–₹24/km for a 12-seat Force Tempo Traveller and ₹24–₹28/km for a 17-seat Tempo Traveller, depending on seat type. Local day rates start from ₹4,500 (100 km/10 hr). WhatsApp for an exact GST quote in 15 minutes.

How many passengers can a Tempo Traveller carry?

Our Force Traveller fleet seats 12, 14, 17, or 26 passengers. Luggage capacity varies — the 12-seater handles one medium bag per person; the 17-seater has overhead bins. If you're unsure which size fits, WhatsApp us the passenger count and we'll recommend.

Can I book a Tempo Traveller for a one-way outstation trip?

Yes. We operate one-way Tempo Traveller trips on all major routes from Ahmedabad — Mumbai, Udaipur, Dwarka, Rann of Kutch, Pune, and more. One-way pricing accounts for empty return; round trips are usually more economical per km. Ask for both quotes.

Is RDB Travels better than booking through Savaari or Ola Outstation?

Savaari and Ola Outstation are aggregators — they connect you to third-party operators, and quality varies. RDB Travels owns its Tempo Traveller fleet and employs its drivers. You book directly with the operator who has been running these vehicles since 2015. No middleman, no surprise subcontracting.

Do you handle wedding and event Tempo Traveller bookings?

Yes — wedding baraats, airport pickups for outstation guests, and fleet coordination across a 3-day event are common bookings. We can provide multiple TTs simultaneously for a large wedding. Advance booking of 10+ days is preferred for wedding-date vehicles.

What is included in the hire price?

Per-km rate, driver bata (allowance), state outstation permit, and GST — all itemised. Toll charges are extra at actuals. Parking at destination is extra. There is no platform fee or broker commission because you're booking directly with the operator.
