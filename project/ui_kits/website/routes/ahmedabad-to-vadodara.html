Route

Fast, simple, reliable

The Ahmedabad–Vadodara Expressway is one of India's better-maintained highway sections. Most sedans and SUVs cruise it in 85–100 minutes on a clear weekday morning. The toll at the Vadodara end costs approximately ₹155 for a sedan.

Vadodara Airport (BDQ) is 10–15 minutes from the expressway exit. If you're catching the IndiGo or Air India shuttle to Mumbai or Delhi, a cab from Ahmedabad to BDQ is often more practical than flying out of SVPI (saving the hassle of Ahmedabad airport traffic).

Common booking types on this route:

Business meetings at GIDC Makarpura or Vadodara city offices — morning trip, 2–3 hour meeting, back by evening. Vadodara Railway Station pickup for guests arriving from Mumbai or Delhi who need onward travel to Ahmedabad. BDQ Airport drop for Ahmedabad residents flying out of Vadodara to avoid SVPI congestion.

Family visits are the other high-volume booking — many Ahmedabad families have relatives in Vadodara, and a cab is faster and more convenient than the Shatabdi or local bus.

FAQ

Honest answers, upfront.

Everything you'd want to know before you book — answered directly.

How much does Ahmedabad to Vadodara cab cost?+

Sedan from ₹11/km — approximately ₹1,210 base fare, plus expressway toll (~₹155 for sedan), driver bata (~₹200), and 5% GST. One of our most affordable outstation runs.

How long does it take?+

~90 minutes on the Ahmedabad–Vadodara Expressway (NH 48) under normal weekday conditions. Rush hour leaving Ahmedabad (8–10 am) can add 20–30 minutes.

Do you do BDQ Airport drops?+

Yes — Vadodara Airport (BDQ) drops from Ahmedabad are a regular booking. Driver plans departure backwards from your boarding time. Flight delays? WhatsApp us — we adjust.

Can I book a return same-day?+

Yes. Most business trips to Vadodara are same-day returns. You go in the morning and we collect you in the afternoon — round-trip rate is quoted upfront and is more economical per km than two one-ways.

Is a cab from Ahmedabad to Vadodara better than the train?+

For a group of 3–4, a cab works out cheaper than 4 train tickets when you factor in convenience. Direct pickup from your door, no transfer to/from station, luggage flexibility, and door-to-door in 90 minutes.

Can I be picked up from SVPI Airport on the way to Vadodara?+

Yes — if you're landing at SVPI Ahmedabad and need to go to Vadodara, we pick up at arrivals and drive straight down. Common for Mumbai shuttle passengers.
